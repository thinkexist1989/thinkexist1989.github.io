Inside ROCOS

From EtherCAT devices to a visual teach pendant.
Explore three connected components.

Choose a component to explore its role.

Architecture schematic · View original diagram

Connect the devices.

ROCOS-ECM is the EtherCAT master. It loads network topology from ENI files and provides access to bus data and servo drives.

It supplies the device-communication layer used by ROCOS-APP.

Explore ROCOS-ECM ↗

Plan and control motion.

ROCOS-APP configures serial robots through URDF and provides robot motion, kinematics, and dynamics capabilities. Parallel mechanisms require custom algorithm modules.

Direct C++ and remote gRPC interfaces connect robot applications to the controller.

Explore ROCOS-APP ↗

See and teach motion.

ROCOS-VIZ is a visual teach pendant with remote connection, robot visualization, curve display, and jog teaching.

The interface connects to the robot-control layer. Recorded demonstrations are available on the project page.

About me

I work at the intersection
of software and robotics.

I'm Yang Luo, a robotics researcher at the Shenyang Institute of Automation, Chinese Academy of Sciences. I received my Ph.D. in 2019 from the State Key Laboratory of Robotics and Systems at Harbin Institute of Technology.

I enjoy designing robot architectures, programming motion, and contributing to open-source tools. My work connects robot system integration, motion control, trajectory planning, human–robot interaction, and dexterous manipulation.
